Evolution & The Removed Experiment

How ArchitectOS Evolved from Prompt Theatre to Deterministic Verification

🚫 The Removed Experiment

Unconstrained Agent Chat Debate: We tested letting agents debate in open conversational chat.

  • Exploded token costs by +380%.
  • Introduced sycophancy (agents politely agreed with hallucinations).
  • Decision: Removed free-form debate.

✅ The Winning Architecture

Typed Contracts & Deterministic Tool Linters:

  • SqlDdlValidator: In-memory DDL syntax & FK integrity.
  • OpenApiValidator: OpenAPI 3.0 route & auth verification.
  • SecurityLinter: Adversarial SAST scanner for rate-limiting.
